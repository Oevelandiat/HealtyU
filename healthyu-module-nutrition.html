Nutrition

Module 2 of 5 2 subsections · 1 activity ~7 min read

Between classes and deadlines, food often becomes an afterthought. This module looks at simple ways to eat well and stay hydrated without needing extra time you don't have.

Subsection 1

Balanced Nutrition

A balanced meal doesn't require complicated recipes — combining a protein source, whole grains, vegetables or fruit, and some healthy fat covers most nutritional needs.

Skipping meals to keep studying often backfires: blood sugar dips can bring on fatigue and difficulty concentrating within a couple of hours.

Keeping a few easy, ready-to-eat options on hand — fruit, nuts, yogurt — helps avoid relying only on vending machines between classes.

Subsection 2

Hydration

Even mild dehydration — before you feel thirsty — can measurably reduce concentration and short-term memory during study sessions.

Carrying a reusable bottle and refilling it between classes is a simple habit that adds up over a full day on campus.
