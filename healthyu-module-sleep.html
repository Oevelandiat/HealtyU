Sleep

Module 1 of 5 2 subsections · 1 activity ~8 min read

Sleep shapes memory, focus and mood — three things university life depends on. This module looks at why sleep matters and how to build a routine that survives exam weeks.

Subsection 1

Why Sleep Matters

Sleep is when the brain consolidates what was studied during the day, turning short-term information into lasting memory.

Consistently short or irregular sleep is linked to slower concentration, lower mood stability and weaker academic performance over a semester.

Most adults need close to eight hours a night — the goal here isn't perfection, but noticing patterns worth adjusting.

Subsection 2

Healthy Sleep Routine

A steady sleep and wake time — even on weekends — does more for sleep quality than the total number of hours alone.

Screens, caffeine late in the day, and irregular study sessions right before bed are common disruptors during university life.
