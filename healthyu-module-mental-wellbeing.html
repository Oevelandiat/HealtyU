Mental Well-being

Module 4 of 5 2 subsections · 1 activity ~7 min read

Deadlines, exams and a full course load can add up. This module looks at how academic stress shows up and a few simple ways to work with it, not against it.

Subsection 1

Academic Stress

Some stress before an exam is normal and can even sharpen focus — it becomes a concern when it's constant, or starts affecting sleep, appetite or motivation.

Overloaded schedules, unclear deadlines and comparing progress with classmates are common academic stress triggers worth noticing.

Recognizing early signs — irritability, trouble concentrating, tension headaches — makes it easier to respond before a stressful period builds up.

Source: institutional student mental-health references (see full citations in-app).

Subsection 2

Relaxation Techniques

Simple techniques — slow breathing, brief mindfulness pauses, progressive muscle relaxation — can lower physical tension within a few minutes.

None of these require special training or equipment; the value comes from practicing them regularly, not only during high-stress moments.
