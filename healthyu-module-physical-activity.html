Physical Activity

Module 3 of 5 2 subsections · 1 activity ~6 min read

Long study sessions mean long stretches of sitting. This module looks at low-effort ways to bring movement back into a packed academic schedule.

Subsection 1

Daily Movement

Extended sitting — common during exam periods — is linked to stiffness, lower energy and reduced focus, independent of formal exercise habits.

Movement doesn't need a gym: walking to class instead of taking transport, or standing during phone calls, adds up over a semester.

Health guidelines generally point to about 150 minutes of moderate activity a week — spread across the week, this is closer to a short daily walk than a major time commitment.

Source: institutional physical-activity guidelines (see full citations in-app).

Subsection 2

Active Study Breaks

A short break every 45–60 minutes of focused study — even just two or three minutes of movement — helps sustain concentration for the next block.

Stretching, a quick walk around the room, or a few bodyweight movements are enough; the goal is interrupting sitting, not fitting in a workout.
